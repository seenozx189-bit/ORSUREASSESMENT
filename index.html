03 / Design Rationale

Olive Leaves

(OLIVE TREE)
Olea europaea

The leaves of Olive tree (Olea europaea) are slender, elongated, & tapering to a point, pale, leathery, and in muted tones. Olive leaves are silvery-underneath and lance-shaped, with a smooth surface and silvery-green undertones — which complements the premium, natural and organic look of the packaging.

COLOR PALETTE INSPIRED BY OLIVE LEAVES
DEEP OLIVE
SAGE GREEN
SILVER GREEN
WARM BEIGE
IVORY
NATURE INSPIRES GOODNESS

Why the Olive Leaf?

The olive leaf was selected as the primary botanical motif because it directly connects the visual language to olive oil and the natural, premium positioning of the product. Its long, narrow form and muted green/grey-green tones also complement the understated botanical aesthetic of the packaging.
